How the scores are worked out

Each question uses a 1–5 scale (1 = lowest, 5 = highest). The average is the mean of all answers. The percentage counts answers of 4 or 5: "Very" or "Extremely useful" for usefulness, and "Agree" or "Strongly agree" for the other questions.

Source: Digital Dojo Café feedback form, 14 responses. Names, emails and response IDs removed.
